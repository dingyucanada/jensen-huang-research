# 2. Biography and documented leadership practice

The Computer History Museum's 2024 Fellow profile records that Jensen Huang was born in Taiwan in 1963, studied electrical engineering at Oregon State and Stanford, and co-founded NVIDIA in 1993 with Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem. Organizational histories should track the contributions of all three founders and of later technical teams. [CLM-013](../sources/evidence.md#clm-013)

At Stanford GSB in 2024, Huang described reasoning from underlying mechanisms when faced with unfamiliar decisions. At Stripe Sessions the same year, he described a CEO reporting structure of roughly 60 people and argued that wider information access helped reduce hierarchy. These statements establish his account at two public interviews. The current reporting count, actual delegation and effectiveness require further evidence such as an organizational record or independently corroborated examples. [CLM-014](../sources/evidence.md#clm-014)

## A protocol for studying decisions

Use the decision episode as the unit of analysis. Record the date, speaker, audience, decision, alternatives known at the time and observable outcome. Code technical reasoning, risk, delegation and coordination independently. Look for failed initiatives and disagreements across accounts so that later success does not determine which evidence enters the sample. Publish source locators and sampling rules. Leave psychological diagnoses and nonpublic personal information out of the corpus.
