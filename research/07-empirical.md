# 7. Financial evidence and market comparisons

The annual series combines the three-year tables in two annual filings. The [reproducible metrics](../docs/metrics.md) report revenue composition, R&D intensity and compound growth. An investor or researcher comparing this series with a peer should align fiscal calendars and classifications before joining the data. Q3 FY2027 is recorded separately, with Data Center rounded at the precision of the earnings release. [CLM-009](../sources/evidence.md#clm-009) · [CLM-010](../sources/evidence.md#clm-010)

## November price window

From the November 20 close to the November 23 close, NVDA gained **31.48%**, SPY **4.71%** and SOXX **13.63%**. NVDA gained **17.85 percentage points** more than SOXX over those closes. NVDA remained **11.57%** below its November 6 close. The annual financial series comes from EDGAR. These market observations use checked closing prices from ChartExchange and are supplemented with AP coverage of the wider move. ## What a formal event study would require

Pre-register announcement times and trading windows. Use a pre-event estimation period, document splits and distributions, and estimate expected returns with more than one benchmark. Test a sector series that excludes NVIDIA and disclose the weights of any constituent-based comparison. Account for the November 17 earnings release and concurrent policy and market news. Report sensitivity to alternative windows and an uncertainty estimate with assumptions. The published comparison uses three closes per symbol and yields a descriptive benchmark difference.
