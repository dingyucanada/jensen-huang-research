# 3. Founder authority and corporate governance

NVIDIA's 2026 definitive proxy reported Huang's beneficial ownership at **870,604,104 common shares, or 3.58%, on March 23, 2026**. The proxy describes a point-in-time measure under SEC beneficial-ownership rules and identifies applicable footnotes and the share denominator. The May proxy also describes nine of ten directors as independent under Nasdaq standards and the then-existing lead-independent-director structure. [CLM-008](../sources/evidence.md#clm-008)

The November filings establish changes to formally held positions. In the first filing NVIDIA disclosed the board's acceptance of Huang's resignation and its appointments of Colette Kress as interim CEO and Harvey Jones as chair. The second filing reported Huang's reinstatement. Jones remained chair and Kress continued as CFO. [CLM-001](../sources/evidence.md#clm-001) · [CLM-002](../sources/evidence.md#clm-002)

## What would indicate dependence on one executive?

Track continuity in product decisions, the allocation of investment authority, senior-team retention and major partner commitments through transitions. Board and executive titles establish formal roles. Independent evidence of who made decisions and how partners responded would help measure informal authority. The brief 2026 transition establishes a small observation window for long-cycle measures such as product delivery.
