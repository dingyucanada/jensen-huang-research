# 6. The November 2026 leadership transition

**Reviewed through November 26, 2026.** The formal leadership record and the procedural status of the inquiry are distinct.

| Event or disclosure | What the public record establishes |
|---|---|
| November 8 | NVIDIA announced an independent-director inquiry in response to press reporting. [CLM-003](../sources/evidence.md#clm-003) |
| November 11 effective, November 12 public | The board accepted Huang's resignation as president, CEO and director, named Kress interim CEO and appointed Jones board chair. [CLM-001](../sources/evidence.md#clm-001) |
| November 17 | Jones said on the earnings call that the board had no intention at that time of reinstating Huang. [CLM-022](../sources/evidence.md#clm-022) |
| November 22 effective, November 23 public | The board reinstated Huang as president, CEO and director. The November 23 release describes the return as immediate. The 8-K specifies November 22 as the effective date. [CLM-002](../sources/evidence.md#clm-002) |
| November 23 | NVIDIA said the inquiry would continue, disclosed a National Security and Export Control Committee and announced a search for a chief compliance officer. [CLM-003](../sources/evidence.md#clm-003) |
| November 24–25 | Members of Congress called for scrutiny. Senator Booker's November 25 request was also covered by Reuters. [CLM-020](../sources/evidence.md#clm-020) · [CLM-007](../sources/evidence.md#clm-007) |

## Inquiry and decision-making

The company acknowledged input from senior U.S. government officials before the decision. Independent coverage contextualizes this account. The public documents establish the board's reported action and statement. A causal account of how the decision was reached would require further evidence such as meeting records or sworn testimony. [CLM-004](../sources/evidence.md#clm-004)

The record reviewed for this edition contains a board statement that the inquiry will continue. Claims about individuals' private conduct, foreign-intelligence activity, legal responsibility and ultimate findings are omitted. Official announcements of an inquiry and oversight requests support conclusions about the procedures they describe.
