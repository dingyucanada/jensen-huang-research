# 8. Findings and research agenda

The evidence establishes three anchors for further work. NVIDIA disclosed a concentrated Data Center revenue mix, a dated minority beneficial ownership position for its founder and a short sequence of board actions that changed his executive and director roles. These address different questions. Together they motivate research into how technical capability, institutional authority and economic dependence interact. [Financials](../sources/evidence.md#clm-009) · [Ownership](../sources/evidence.md#clm-008) · [Reinstatement](../sources/evidence.md#clm-002)

| Hypothesis | Test | A result that would weaken it |
|---|---|---|
| Customers absorb materially higher costs when switching accelerator ecosystems | Compare paired deployments for the same workload, including engineering and validation hours | Teams move production work at similar cost and reliability |
| Founder-led coordination is important for continuity | Compare decision authority, executive retention and partner actions across comparable transitions | Delivery and commitments hold through a prolonged succession |
| Infrastructure providers influence policy through technical information | Date submissions and compare them with agency explanations and final provisions | Agencies consistently adopt a different explanation and policy design |
