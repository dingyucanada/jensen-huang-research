# Community conduct

Treat contributors and subjects with respect. Critique claims using evidence. Harassment, threats, identity-based attacks, unwanted contact and disclosure of private information are unacceptable. Keep comments relevant and distinguish a person's public role from their private life.

Maintainers may remove harmful material, close abusive discussions and restrict participation. To report misconduct, contact a maintainer privately through a contact method listed in the maintainer's profile. If a private contact is unavailable, request a private channel in a public issue without including identifying details. Maintainers should acknowledge reports, limit access to the people handling them and explain applicable decisions to affected contributors when possible.
