## How to update the tracker

For a rule, record the Federal Register document number, publication date, effective date and comment date if each is stated. Use the legal text and agency notice together when an announcement precedes publication. Record corrections and rescissions as separate dated observations with links to the prior item.

For congressional oversight, check committee agendas and posted testimony before changing a scheduled event. Use a rescheduled, held or canceled status only with a dated official record. Record the date and supporting link. A press statement can support attribution. A subpoena or request establishes a procedural action. Document any subsequent determination with its own source and date.

The repository includes an opt-in [Federal Register review queue](../watch/README.md#federal-register-intake). Candidate results require editorial review before any public tracker update.
