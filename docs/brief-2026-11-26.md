# Developments through November 26, 2026

NVIDIA's November 23 Form 8-K says the board reinstated Jensen Huang effective November 22 after accepting his resignation effective November 11. The November 23 announcement also said the independent-director inquiry would continue and described new compliance oversight. [Departure](../sources/evidence.md#clm-001) · [Reinstatement](../sources/evidence.md#clm-002) · [Inquiry status](../sources/evidence.md#clm-003)

- **November 17.** NVIDIA reported Q3 FY2027 consolidated revenue of $115.844B, including about $107.6B in Data Center revenue, for the quarter ended October 25. [Earnings record](../sources/evidence.md#clm-010)
- **November 19.** BIS published an interim final rule addressing remote access to advanced computing. [Published rule](../sources/evidence.md#clm-023)
- **November 24.** House Foreign Affairs Ranking Member Gregory Meeks called for a public hearing. Five senators separately released a statement about the reinstatement. [Congressional statements](../sources/evidence.md#clm-020)
- **November 25.** Senator Cory Booker requested information from NVIDIA's board chair about the reinstatement and competition concerns. [Senator's request and Reuters coverage](../sources/evidence.md#clm-007)
- **December 1 and 2, scheduled.** House committee chairs announced transcribed interviews for Huang and NVIDIA General Counsel Tim Teter, respectively. [House notice](../sources/evidence.md#clm-005)
- **December 3, scheduled.** The Senate Banking Committee listed Huang for a 10 a.m. Washington, D.C. public hearing on U.S. export controls. [Senate notice](../sources/evidence.md#clm-006)
