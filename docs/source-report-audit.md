# Audit of the source report

This edition draws on the structure of the November 24, 2026 Jensen Huang research report. The Word and PDF editions were inspected together, including the tables, notes and six appendices. Claims carried into this edition were independently checked against English sources. The release scope is the evidence ledger, the chapter text and the datasets shipped here.

| Original topic | Treatment in this release |
|---|---|
| Chapters 1–2 and biography appendix | Retained the research question, documented education and founding, and two direct interview accounts. Narrowed personality language to observable statements. Left fine-grained childhood, career and biographical anecdotes outside the public scope. |
| Chapter 3 and proxy disclosures | Checked the March 23 beneficial ownership figure and the May proxy's historical board characterization. Kept both date-specific. |
| Chapter 4 and financial appendix | Checked the six annual observations, R&D and Data Center revenue. Rebuilt the ratios and retained quarterly figures as a separate table. CUDA, AlexNet and Mellanox entries have source-specific locators. |
| Chapter 5 and policy appendix | Checked the published 2022, 2023 and 2026 rules, the issuer's H20 accounting disclosure, the Arm merger inquiry and the CSIS transcript. The public citation for the Arm inquiry now uses the UK Competition and Markets Authority's announcement. Used the January 15 final rule date for the effective policy change. |
| Chapter 6 and governance appendix | Verified the board actions with Item 5.02 filings and issuer releases. Attributed the disclosed inquiry and government input. Omitted private allegations and unresolved claims of wrongdoing. Included later public notices and the November 25 oversight request. |
| Chapter 6 and market appendix | Rechecked the selected closes used in published endpoint comparisons. SOXX on November 23 displays **$140.22** in the current source, compared with **$140.26** in the prior report. This revision produces **13.63%** for SOXX and a **17.85 percentage point** NVDA–SOXX difference. The full original price and volume table was left outside the release. |
| Chapters 7–8 and research appendices | Kept the testable research questions. Treated formal causal estimates, longitudinal comparisons and full interview coding as research designs. The original reading list was narrowed to sources used in this edition. |
